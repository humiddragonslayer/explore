AKA: The Lost Citadel of the Scarlet Minotaur, The Minotaur's Keep, Ärrgivare (Scargiver)

A castle older than memory, with a giant scarlet minotaur roaming its halls. The Shadowdark seeps through its walls, wafting away in the open air. It does not have a fixed location, breaking through the earth in a fresh place every time, sometimes unseen for a few decades (though whether that is because it went somewhere uninhabited or whether it left the planet entirely is a question left to stronger minds). 

It was thought to be the creation of an ancient [[Necromancer]] as his home base, but its resilience beyond his death has proven that it predates him. Further, the few accounts of the place that survive to see the outside world often conflict on their details, the size and number of rooms and what they contain. One thing, however, is certain: there is treasure beyond recognition within it.

Every time it leaves a location to go back into the Earth, it leaves behind it a scar that does not fade (or at least has not faded in centuries of recorded history). The scarred earth cannot be dug or mined, though removing the scar as a whole has not been tried, due to the scar being as big as a castle in terms of square footage.
