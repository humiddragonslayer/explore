A goblin rogue with a penchant for nudism while alive, he was killed in battle in one of the early adventures into the Lost Citadel of the Scarlet Minotaur. Brought back by the [[Necromancer]], he was effectively immortal while his flesh rotted away with time, leaving an animated goblin skeleton. He lounges around the Citadel, whiling away his time finding alternate heads, and looking for fellow skeletons to make friends (though they aren't nearly as talkative).

Originally played by Joel Vilan
