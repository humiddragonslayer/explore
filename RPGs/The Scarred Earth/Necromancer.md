The BBEG of LCotSM Campaign 1, who was trying to clear the dungeon to safely enter, because he was bound by a curse that he couldn't cast magic that could harm. He needed to get to the heart of the [[Citadel]] to regain his full power and destroy the curse, and posed as a kindly old wizard to convince adventurers to do it for him. Various parties tried and finally succeeded in clearing it, which led to him reborn at full power, relocating the Citadel to the centre of the city to sacrifice the entire population and power up to then take over the capital.

However, the party saw this happening from the city itself and teleported to where he was hovering above the Citadel mid ritual, and managed to drag him back to the ground. With a lot of luck and a little bit of courage, they managed to wound his still-powering-up self and finally kill him, triggering the Citadel's fall back into the earth.

The End?
