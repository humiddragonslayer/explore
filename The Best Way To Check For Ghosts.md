The best way to check for ghosts is to write something offensive in invisible ink. That way, if anything untoward happens, you'll know it's because they read your rude message. You must, however, take care that you are not too rude, for that might lead to a ghost inflicted injury that might be a bit too much of a hassle to recover from. Some choose to avoid this problem entirely by writing something positive. However, the nature of humans to give credit for misfortune and take it for the alternative means that there have been a few cases of ghoulish jealousy brimming to the point that it might as well have been an insult that led to the final outcome.
